# 04 Making it sound heavenly

> 🔎 **[Searchbar](../../Searchbar.md)** — find any doc, setting, function or GitHub page (Ctrl+F)

[TONE and SPACE](../Devices/TONE%20and%20SPACE.md) is the purple unit near the top. Its **POWER** knob has three positions:

- **OFF** — does nothing
- **TONE** — polish only
- **LOUDNESS** — polish plus width and room

## TONE

| Knob | What it does |
|---|---|
| SMOOTH | calms harsh ringing, only where and when it happens |
| AIR | brightness on top |
| WARMTH | weight in the low mids |
| BODY | makes thin sounds whole |
| SUB | a soft extra low end that swells between bass notes |
| OUTPUT | volume |

Buttons: **PROTECT** (keeps hits sharp), **TAPE** (tape warmth), **MATCH** (keeps the volume the same so you judge the sound, not the loudness).

## SPACE

| Knob | What it does |
|---|---|
| WIDTH | how wide the stereo is (100 % = unchanged) |
| SPACE | how much room you hear |
| DECAY | how long the room rings |
| SHIMMER | a sparkly octave-up in the room |
| TONE | dark or bright room |

Buttons: **DUCK** (room gets out of the way of loud sounds), **BASS MONO** (keeps bass in the middle), **MOD** (a slowly moving room).

**AUTO** listens to the music and picks the SPACE settings for you. **HEAVEN** sets how much it may take over.

## For games

Start at LOUDNESS. Turn **SPACE** up until the world feels open, but you can still tell where a step
came from. If the room blurs directions, raise **DUCK** first. Keep **WIDTH** at 140 % or less.

For music on a bus, TONE alone is the safe choice.
