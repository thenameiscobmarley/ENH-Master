# AUDIO EXCITER & MAXIMIZER

> 🔎 **[Searchbar](../../Searchbar.md)** — find any doc, setting, function or GitHub page (Ctrl+F)

Formerly called 882i.

A professional audio exciter and "sonic maximizer" of the classic 1990s kind (1U, model SONIC MAXIMIZER).
Speakers, and long processing chains, delay the lows behind the highs, which smears every attack. The
882i lines them up again, then adds clarity where the top is quiet. It starts in the
[Gear locker](../UI/Gear%20locker.md). **POWER** off: it doesn't touch the sound.

- **LO CONTOUR**: 0 to 10. Lifts the lows (under 150 Hz) by up to +12 dB. A 25 Hz subsonic filter is always in, as on the hardware.
- **PROCESS**: 0 to 10. The highs lead: the mids are held back up to 0.5 ms and the lows up to 2.5 ms, so an attack arrives as one. The top (above 2.4 kHz) is lifted by up to +12 dB, less the brighter the sound already is: dull material gets clarity, bright material is left alone.
- **OUTPUT**: ±12 dB.

The three bands are split so they add back flat (Linkwitz-Riley at 150 Hz and 2.4 kHz). Zero latency:
the high band is never delayed. Its LED ladder shows how much it is lifting.

Code: `DSP/units/Exciter.h`, panel: `Tools/units/gen_units.py`. Tests: `EnhDspTests --units16`.
