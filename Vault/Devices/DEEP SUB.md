# DEEP SUB

> 🔎 **[Searchbar](../../Searchbar.md)** — find any doc, setting, function or GitHub page (Ctrl+F)

Low end you feel: deep, dark and huge, like standing in a steel hull under water. It *adds* sound
rather than boosting what's there.

## Knobs

- **DEPTH** — follows the bass line and adds a note one octave lower. Notes that would go too low to
  hear are strengthened at their own pitch instead.
- **HULL** — six deep resonances, like a steel shell, that ring after every hit.
- **SIZE** — from a small boat to a huge hull (lower and longer).
- **PRESSURE** — weight you can feel even on small speakers.
- **IN** — on or off. **SUB** meter — how much it's adding.

## Safe by design

What it adds is mono, never goes below 18 Hz, and backs off when the music is already near full
volume. The limiters after it look after the rest. At 0 it doesn't touch the sound at all.

## In its glass panel

**SUB SHAPE** (sine, warm, growl) · **TRACKING** (standard, fast, stable) · **HULL MATERIAL** (steel, iron, cavern).

Try the **DEEP SUB: SUBMARINE** preset. Code: `DeepSub.h`.
