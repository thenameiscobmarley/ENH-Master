# TONE and SPACE

> 🔎 **[Searchbar](../../Searchbar.md)** — find any doc, setting, function or GitHub page (Ctrl+F)

The purple unit near the top. It takes everything the rack did and makes it sound finished.
How to use it: [Making it sound heavenly](../Tutorial/04%20Making%20it%20sound%20heavenly.md).

## The front

- **POWER** — OFF, TONE, or TONE + SPACE.
- **TONE** knobs: SMOOTH, AIR, WARMTH, BODY, SUB, OUTPUT. Buttons: PROTECT, TAPE, MATCH.
- **SPACE** knobs: WIDTH, SPACE, DECAY, SHIMMER, TONE. Buttons: DUCK, BASS MONO, MOD.
- **AUTO** + **HEAVEN** — let it choose the SPACE settings by listening; HEAVEN is how much it may.
- **MULTIPLY / STRENGTH** — the unit's masters.

## Its screen

Left: the harsh spots SMOOTH is calming right now. Right: a left/right bar for each effect, so you
see what's happening to each channel.

## Good to know

- **BASS MONO** keeps bass in the middle. Bass that's out of phase (like rear effects in a surround
  game) is folded into the middle too, not thrown away.
- TONE runs at double speed inside so bright sounds stay clean (1 ms of delay).
- Its loudness hold is slow and ignores bass, so it doesn't pump.
